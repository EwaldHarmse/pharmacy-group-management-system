# Pharmacy Prescription Management System

Systems analysis and design for **HealthCare Plus Pharmacy**, a community pharmacy in Pretoria, South Africa that still handles prescriptions in paper logbooks and verifies them with doctors by phone. The project proposes a digital Prescription Management System to cut wait times, reduce dispensing errors and keep a reliable prescription history.

The full write-up is in [`PharmacyGroupManagementSystem.docx`](PharmacyGroupManagementSystem.docx).

## Contents

- **Business background**: the pharmacy, the problem area, the target user (head pharmacist) and how requirements were gathered (interviews, observation, record review)
- **Mission, vision and PIECES analysis**: Performance, Information, Economy, Control, Efficiency, Service
- **Business rules and system functions**: entities and attributes for Patient, Doctor, Prescription, Medication, Medical Aid, Sale, Payment and more
- **Modelling**: ERD, DFD and use case diagrams
- **Normalisation** of the data model
- **Reports**: detailed, summary and exception reports
- **Data conversion plan**: scope, data to convert, approach, roles, risk management and post-conversion activities
